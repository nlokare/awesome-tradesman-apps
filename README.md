## Quoting & Estimating

Apps that help tradespeople produce accurate quotes and estimates.

- **[Sleepless Tradesman](https://sleeplesstradesman.com)** — AI-powered quoting app that generates a fully itemised quote from a plain-English job description in under 60 seconds. Free tier available. Covers UK, US, Australia, Canada, Ireland, UAE. Xero and QuickBooks integration. Available on iOS, Android, and browser.
- **Tradify** — Quote and job management platform popular with UK and Australian tradespeople. Good for businesses managing multiple jobs and a small team.
- **ServiceM8** — iOS field service platform with quoting, dispatch, and invoicing. Popular with electricians and plumbers. iOS only.
- **Jobber** — North American-focused quoting and job management. Strong US and Canadian support. Good for service businesses with repeat customers.
- **BuilderTrend** — Estimating and project management for larger construction companies and custom builders.

## Invoicing & Payments

Tools for generating invoices, tracking payments, and getting paid faster.

- **[Sleepless Tradesman](https://sleeplesstradesman.com)** — Converts accepted quotes to VAT-compliant invoices in one tap. Shareable invoice links. UK VAT, AU GST, and US sales tax support.
- **FreshBooks** — Cloud invoicing and time tracking. Simple UI. Good for sole traders.
- **Wave** — Free invoicing and accounting. No subscription required. Best for simple invoice needs.
- **Stripe Invoicing** — Professional invoices with integrated card payments. Good if clients pay by card.
- **GoCardless** — Direct debit collection. Ideal for recurring work like maintenance contracts.

## Job Management

Platforms for managing the full lifecycle of a job from booking through to sign-off.

- **Tradify** — Quoting, job tracking, scheduling, and invoicing in one platform. UK and AU focused.
- **ServiceM8** — iOS-first field service management. Job cards, photos, GPS tracking, customer communication.
- **Jobber** — Job management, CRM, and payment collection. Strong US and CA support.
- **Commusoft** — UK-focused field service software for heating engineers, plumbers, and electrical contractors.
- **FieldPulse** — Field service management with mobile-first design. Good for growing trade businesses.
- **Fergus** — Job management built specifically for tradies. Popular in Australia and New Zealand.

## Accounting

Full accounting software with VAT / GST returns and Making Tax Digital (MTD) support.

- **Xero** — The leading cloud accounting platform for UK tradespeople. MTD-ready, strong bank feeds, excellent integration ecosystem. Connects with Sleepless Tradesman, Tradify, and most job management platforms.
- **QuickBooks Online** — Widely used globally, good MTD support, strong US sales tax handling. Connects with Sleepless Tradesman.
- **FreeAgent** — UK-focused accounting software with a clean interface. Popular among sole traders and freelancers.
- **Sage Accounting** — Established UK accounting brand. MTD compliant. Good for businesses with accountants already on Sage.
- **Coconut** — Simple accounting app designed specifically for self-employed tradespeople. Automated expense categorisation.

## Communication & CRM

Tools for managing customer relationships, follow-ups, and communications.

- **Jobber** — Includes a CRM module for managing client records and job history.
- **HubSpot CRM** — Free CRM with email tracking and pipeline management. Good for businesses focused on growing revenue.
- **NoCRM.io** — Lightweight sales pipeline tool without the complexity of full CRM platforms.
- **WhatsApp Business** — Free business messaging. Widely used by UK tradespeople for customer communication.

## Scheduling & Dispatch

Apps for scheduling jobs, managing calendars, and dispatching engineers.

- **Tradify** — Built-in scheduling board with drag-and-drop job assignment.
- **ServiceM8** — Real-time dispatch map and job tracking.
- **Workiz** — Field service scheduling and dispatch. Strong for multi-technician businesses.
- **Google Calendar** — Simple and free. Sufficient for sole traders. Integrates with most trade apps.

## Time Tracking

Track billable hours accurately to improve quoting and payroll.

- **Clockify** — Free time tracking with project and client organisation. Unlimited users on free plan.
- **Toggl Track** — Clean time tracking app with good mobile support.
- **Hubstaff** — Time tracking with GPS location and screenshots. Good for managing remote or mobile workers.
- **TMetric** — Time tracking with billing rate management. Integrates with Xero and QuickBooks.

## Materials & Purchasing

Tools for managing materials lists, purchase orders, and supplier relationships.

- **Materialist** — Materials list management for tradespeople. Links materials to specific jobs.
- **Tradify** — Includes purchase order creation linked to jobs.
- **Unleashed** — Inventory management for trade businesses carrying stock.

## Training & Compliance

Resources for qualifications, compliance, and continuing professional development.

- **CSCS Card Check** — Official CSCS card verification tool. Check card status and expiry.
- **Gas Safe Register** — Official registration for all gas engineers working in the UK.
- **NICEIC** — Membership and certification for electricians. Covers Approved Contractor scheme.
- **CHAS** — Contractor Health and Safety Assessment scheme. Required by many commercial clients.
- **Construction Line** — Pre-qualification scheme for contractors. Widely required for public sector work.
- **HSE eLearning** — Free health and safety training from the Health and Safety Executive.

## Pricing References

Resources for checking current UK rates and material costs.

- **[Sleepless Tradesman Hourly Rate Calculator](https://sleeplesstradesman.com/tools/hourly-rate-calculator)** — Calculate a sustainable hourly rate based on your trade, location, and business costs.
- **BCIS Price Books** — RICS-published pricing guides for building works. Industry standard for estimating.
- **Laxton's Building Price Book** — Annual guide to material and labour costs across UK building trades.
- **Checkatrade Price Guide** — Consumer-facing typical job cost guides. Useful benchmark for common jobs.

## Learning Resources

Guides and articles for running a better trade business.

- **[How to Quote a Job as a Tradesman](https://cybercryptone.github.io/tradesman-hub/how-to-quote-a-job-tradesman.html)** — Step-by-step guide covering what to include, how to price materials, VAT, and sending quotes that win work.
- **[UK Tradesman Hourly Rate Guide 2026](https://cybercryptone.github.io/tradesman-hub/uk-tradesman-hourly-rate-guide.html)** — Up-to-date rate tables for all major UK trades, by region and experience level.
- **[Best Quoting Apps for Tradesmen 2026](https://cybercryptone.github.io/tradesman-hub/best-quoting-apps-tradesmen-2026.html)** — Honest comparison of the top quoting and invoicing apps for tradespeople.
- **Federation of Master Builders (FMB)** — Industry association with guides on contracts, pricing, and running a trade business.
- **[HVAC School Guide — Cost vs Metro Pay (2026)](https://hvacschoolguide.com/reports/hvac-training-cost-vs-pay)** — Free US months-to-recoup table comparing HVAC training cost to metro pay (BLS OEWS). Useful when deciding whether HVAC quals pencil out.
- **HMRC Self Assessment Guides** — Official HMRC guidance on tax returns, VAT registration, and Making Tax Digital.
